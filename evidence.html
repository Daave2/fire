Evidence & Sources

What this record is based on

This page explains the sources behind Out of the Ashes. It separates public verification from resident records, keeps private details out of the public site, and records what is still unknown.

Latest Status: April 19, 2026

The fire happened on February 6, 2026. Firefighters remained at the scene for four days. Residents closest to the site were first told they could return on February 27, but for 4 Gordon Street access did not mean the property was properly livable.

  • The home remains tied up in repair, insurance, habitability, utility and council tax processes.
  • Residents are still seeking clarity about the Waterloo Road end of Gordon Street, where structural reports are understood to be awaited for the flat/building at the end of the street.
  • A formal complaint has been prepared and an Environmental Information Regulations / FOI request has been sent to Blackpool Council seeking records about handover, site safety, fencing, demolition, communication, access and outstanding works.
  • Street condition issues have continued, including rubbish, fly-tipping, demolition debris and requests for cleansing.

How to Read This Record

This site is a resident-created record. Some details are directly verified by official incident logs or public reporting. Other details come from emails, claim correspondence, council tax correspondence, resident notes, photographs, and a private WhatsApp community export. Private material is summarised rather than published in full.

Where a source is not official, this site treats it as supporting evidence rather than final proof. Community reports about structural safety, ownership, demolition plans or the fire cause should be checked against formal records before being relied on.

What Is Firmly Established

  • A major commercial building fire was logged on Waterloo Road at 23:30 on February 6, 2026.
  • LFRS recorded incident number 2602002136 and a major multi-appliance response.
  • Smart Play was publicly reported as destroyed and Smart Mart damaged.
  • Residents near the site were evacuated, and 2, 4 and 6 Gordon Street were among the homes closest to the rear of the fire site.
  • 4 Gordon Street was treated through correspondence as uninhabitable after the fire.
  • Access to the property was not restored until February 27, when the rear gates were gone, the back door was boarded, power was off and the house appeared to have been searched.
  • Recovery issues continued into April, including council tax, VOA, insurance, accommodation, rear access, fencing, street cleansing and communication questions.

Open Questions

  • The confirmed fire cause has not been published in the sources reviewed for this site.
  • The full Fire Service investigation report has not been seen.
  • Formal council handover records, risk assessments, dangerous structure decisions, demolition records, fencing decisions and access decisions are still being requested.
  • The final VOA decision and final council tax relief position are not yet recorded here.
  • The final police outcome for the burglary report is not yet recorded here.
  • The final repair timetable and responsibility for remaining site/access issues are not yet clear.

Evidence Principles

This site aims to be fair, sourced and careful. It does not publish bank details, private phone numbers, current accommodation details, raw WhatsApp messages, private email addresses, or unnecessary third-party personal information. It distinguishes between official/public sources, first-hand resident records, and community reports that still need formal confirmation.

That caution matters. The goal is not to make the story louder. The goal is to make it harder to dismiss.

Corrections and updates: This record is intended to remain source-led. If a confirmed error is found, or if a public body, resident, business, or source provides clearer evidence, the site should be updated to reflect that record rather than preserve an older version of events.
