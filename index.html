Waterloo Road Fire · Blackpool · February 2026

Out of the Ashes

A resident-led record of the fire, displacement, community response and long recovery around Gordon Street.

Latest status Evidence notes

February 6, 2026. Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service aerial ladder platforms tackling the blaze at the Smart Mart commercial building on Waterloo Road.

On the night of February 6, 2026, a major fire broke out at a commercial building on Waterloo Road in Blackpool's South Shore. Fifteen fire engines, aerial ladder platforms, and specialist units responded. Firefighters remained on scene for four days.

The blaze destroyed multiple businesses and displaced neighbouring residents from Gordon Street. What followed was not just a fire, but a prolonged recovery marked by displacement, uncertainty, property damage, insurance disputes, security concerns, council tax and utility problems, and the difficult gap between being allowed back home and actually being able to live there. This site records that story through one resident's experience, the wider community response, and the questions that still matter.

Waterloo Road also has a longer story. Blackpool Council's heritage record identifies it as one of South Shore's early east-west streets and a former commercial spine for the area. The fire therefore sits inside a bigger question: how does South Shore recover from one emergency while still fighting for the investment, public space and everyday care its high streets need?

Latest Status

Updated April 19, 2026. Access to Gordon Street has changed since the first weeks after the fire, but recovery remains unresolved. The home at 4 Gordon Street is still being treated as uninhabitable in correspondence, with insurance, repairs, council tax, rear access, and site-condition questions ongoing.

  • The Waterloo Road end remains a live issue, with residents told structural engineer reports are still awaited for the flat/building at the end of the street.
  • A formal complaint has been prepared for Blackpool Council and an Environmental Information Regulations / FOI request has been sent asking for records about handover, site safety, communication, access, fencing, demolition and outstanding works.
  • Street condition problems have continued, including rubbish, fly-tipping, debris and requests for cleansing around Gordon Street and the surrounding access routes.
